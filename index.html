FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About AI Search Resources

What are AI search optimization resources?

AI search optimization resources are guides, tools, definitions and data that teach you how to get named in AI answers. Our AI SEO learning hub brings them together in one place. You will find a blog, guides, free tools, a glossary, original research and a newsletter. All of it is written in plain words.

Yes. You can read every guide, use every tool and browse the glossary at no cost. You do not need a card. The newsletter is free too. Our only paid work is our services, such as full AI search optimization. You never need to buy anything to use the library.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with the guide What Is AEO? It explains the main idea in plain words. Then read AEO vs GEO vs SEO to see how the terms fit together. After that, run the AI Visibility Checker to see where your business stands today. Use the glossary whenever a word is new.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO focuses on being the direct answer to a question. GEO focuses on being named as a source in answers that AI tools write. They overlap a lot, and many teams use the terms the same way. Our guide AEO vs GEO vs SEO explains each one in plain words.

Do I need an account to use the free tools?

No. The tools are free to open and use. You do not need an account or a card to try them. Each tool gives a quick read of your site or your ads. For a deeper check, book the Free AI Visibility Audit. It looks at more than a quick tool can.

How do I know the advice is current?

Each page shows the date it last changed. We update guides when search changes, and we name the sources behind any study or number. AI search moves fast, so we also share updates on the blog. Join the newsletter to hear about new guides and tools as they launch.

Can Grozix do this work for me?

Yes. Our team runs AI search optimization for clients, from AEO and GEO to schema, content and tracking. You can learn here first and hire us later, or start with the Free AI Visibility Audit. We will tell you plainly what to fix and what it would take.

Where can I find the meaning of an AI search term?

Open the glossary. Each term has its own page with a short, plain meaning. You will find AEO, GEO, LLMO, AI Overview, citation, entity, schema and more. You can also type the term into the search box at the top of this page.
